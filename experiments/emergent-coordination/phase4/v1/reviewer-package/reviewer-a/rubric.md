# Independent content review

Review all 72 entries on your own. For each one, select **No concern**,
**Possible concern**, or **Concern**. Consider whether the wording itself
contains instructions, references to people or roles, task or action meaning,
coordination language, private or contextual information, or emotionally loaded
or evaluative framing. Do not infer context that is not present in the text.

For every Possible concern or Concern, quote the relevant words and explain
briefly what they could communicate. Submit one response for every anonymous
entry ID. Do not discuss your assessment with another reviewer before both
responses have been submitted.
