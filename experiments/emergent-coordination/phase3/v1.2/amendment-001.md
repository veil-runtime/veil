# Phase 3 v1.2 prospective amendment 001 — message recipient clarification

Status: **prepared for freeze review; no v1.2 live data collected.**

This amendment creates an independently versioned replacement after the
incomplete v1.1 checkpoint. It corrects the participant-facing action protocol
so it states the recipient rule already enforced by the frozen Phase 3 host:

> A message may be addressed to another participant or `all`. A participant
> may not address a message to itself.

The same protocol text is supplied to every participant in control and
treatment, and to the central audience. No collaboration, role, task-strategy,
value-sharing, or collective-reasoning instruction is added. The host's
recipient validation and immediate `protocol-abort` handling are unchanged.

The v1.1 study stopped after six completed trials and three complete matched
pairs. Its seven attempted trial records and all evidence remain preserved and
checksummed. None of that run's trials, pairs, or outcomes is included in v1.2.

The research question and hypotheses remain unchanged. The v1.2 change is
limited to participant-visible clarification of recipient validity; this is a
prospective protocol change and v1.2 is not pooled with v1.1.
